Byungjin Cho leads the Network and Decision Systems (NDS) Lab. His research addresses performance limits and trade-offs, network design and resource allocation, and learning and decision systems.

## Background

- **Industry:** Senior Research Scientist at Nokia, Finland; Engineer at SK hynix, Korea.
- **Academic:** Postdoctoral research, doctorate, and master's studies in electrical engineering at Aalto University, Finland.
